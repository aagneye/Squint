Does your vision AI know when it can't read the label? Squint degrades medicine and food labels step by step and finds where reading turns into guessing. A blurry 25 mg bottle must get "please retake the photo", never a confident wrong dose.

All labels are synthetic. Squint tests reading, not treatment, and gives no medical advice.
